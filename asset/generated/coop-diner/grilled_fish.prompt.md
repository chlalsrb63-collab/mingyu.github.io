# 생선구이 (grilled_fish) — 16×16 협동 식당 요리 아이콘

Prompt:
Create a 16x16 pixel-art icon of a whole grilled fish on a platter for a cute isometric MMORPG kitchen mini-game (Juju Diner).
- Silhouette: An asymmetric diagonal whole fish with a pointed snout on the lower-left and a prominent V-notched forked tail fin jutting out at the upper-right, marked with dark diagonal grill sear stripes across its belly, resting on a low oval plate.
- Perspective: 3/4 top-down view, lit from the top-left.
- Palette: ≤ 8 colors from the Tales Tales game palette (roast gold/brown #fff0a0, #c48e5c, #9a6a40, #6a4434; char marks #373a46; plate #e2e8f0, #7a7f98; garnish #a4e07a; 1px outer outline #2b2233).
- Rules: Crisp 1x pixel grid, binary alpha (transparent background), no anti-aliasing, no gradients, no pillow shading.
