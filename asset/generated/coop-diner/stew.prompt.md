# 고기 스튜 (stew) — 16×16 협동 식당 요리 아이콘

Prompt:
Create a 16x16 pixel-art icon of a hot meat stew in a two-handled pot for a cute isometric MMORPG kitchen mini-game (Juju Diner).
- Silhouette: A deep, heavy barrel-shaped pot with two symmetrical side ear-handles protruding left and right, chunky square meat/carrot pieces on the flat broth rim, and two vertical steam wisps rising above.
- Perspective: 3/4 front-top view, lit from the top-left.
- Palette: ≤ 8 colors from the Tales Tales game palette (iron pot #9a9eaa, #5a5e6c, #373a46; broth & chunks #ff9a3a, #e04a3c, #a0283a, #fff0a0; steam #f0f6ff; 1px outer outline #2b2233).
- Rules: Crisp 1x pixel grid, binary alpha (transparent background), no anti-aliasing, no gradients, no pillow shading.
