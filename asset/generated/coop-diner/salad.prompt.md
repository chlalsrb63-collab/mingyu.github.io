# 채소 샐러드 (salad) — 16×16 협동 식당 요리 아이콘

Prompt:
Create a 16x16 pixel-art icon of a fresh vegetable salad for a cute isometric MMORPG kitchen mini-game (Juju Diner).
- Silhouette: Three ruffled, spiky lettuce leaf peaks bursting high above a narrow tapered wooden bowl, so the tall bumpy leaf crown is immediately distinguishable in grayscale from the flat grilled fish and the wide-eared stew pot.
- Perspective: 3/4 front-top view, lit from the top-left.
- Palette: ≤ 8 colors from the Tales Tales game palette (greens #a4e07a, #5aa84a, #2e6a46; tomato/egg #e04a3c, #fff0a0; wooden bowl #c48e5c, #9a6a40, #6a4434; 1px outer outline #2b2233).
- Rules: Crisp 1x pixel grid, binary alpha (transparent background), no anti-aliasing, no gradients, no pillow shading.
