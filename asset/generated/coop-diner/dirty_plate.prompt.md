# 더러운 접시 (dirty_plate) — 16×16 협동 식당 설거지 아이콘

Prompt:
Create a 16x16 pixel-art icon of a dirty plate with leftover scraps for a cute isometric MMORPG kitchen mini-game (Juju Diner).
- Silhouette: A flat dinner plate whose upper-right contour is broken by a sticking-out chewed bone scrap, irregular brown sauce smudges, a wilted green scrap, and a foul stink wisp above.
- Perspective: 3/4 top-down view, lit from the top-left.
- Palette: ≤ 8 colors from the Tales Tales game palette (porcelain #e2e8f0, #b4bcc9, #7a7f98; bone scrap #fff8e4; sauce smudge #94786a, #6a4434; wilted leaf #5aa84a; grime #9a5ae0; 1px outer outline #2b2233).
- Rules: Crisp 1x pixel grid, binary alpha (transparent background), no anti-aliasing, no gradients, no pillow shading.
