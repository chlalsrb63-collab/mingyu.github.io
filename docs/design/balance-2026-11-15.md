# Character balance review — 2026-11-15

Owner's order: "캐릭터 밸런스 … 검토해서 적용해 줘." Measured with the existing bots, then the smallest numeric changes.
Hammer jobs are Codex's (numbers suggested in §7 only); no job added or removed; the 도적's single-target damage untouched.

## 0. Method

- **Bands**: `scripts/balance-probe.mjs probe()` (flat walled arena, three monsters always on, 1.5 s respawn, no potions,
  120 s) for the six non-hammer weapon templates, each tier fed its own regular kinds (the ≤4 nearest within ±1 tier;
  bosses, chests, event kinds left out). Mean of the six. XP/min = kills/min × mean XP (+ the early-hunt bonus below
  Lv.40); minutes/level = `defs().lvc` ÷ XP/min (surcharge past stat 30 not counted: +3–15 %). Hours per band sum the
  levels with XP/min interpolated between probed tiers. **Solo, kills only** (no quests, dailies, streak, party, rift,
  gear `xp`) and a meat-grinder pace: read the numbers relatively, not as wall-clock promises.
- **Survival**: the same runs — damage taken/min ÷ the sword template's max HP, and how many 농축 빨간 물약 (480 + 24 %)
  that pace would need; deaths/h = deaths per 120 s × 30 (no potions, so high).
- **Kill time**: `scripts/skill-probe.mjs` single-target dmg/s (mean of all 19 jobs) against band HP; elite ×3.5 HP,
  field boss ×14 HP (`ELITE`), solo, no dodging.
- **Jobs**: `node scripts/skill-probe.mjs 13 25 40` (pinned dummies; st = one, aoe = six, boss = tier boss dummy).
  Probe fix in this pass: it dressed heroes by `tierFor(4·tier − 3)`, i.e. tier-5 gear (req. Lv.149) at T13/T25;
  now `tierFor(tier)` like `balance-probe.mjs` (T40 unchanged; T13/T25 numbers below are the first fair ones).

## 2. Cliffs and flats found

1. **Lv.181–200 speed-up (cliff, fixed).** The six tier-47–50 kinds paid 3,100–3,950 XP (XP/HP 0.73–0.95 against 0.43–0.63
   for tiers 40–46) while a level's price rises only 3 %/tier: minutes/level fell 31 → 23 for the last 20 levels.
2. **Lv.69–109 ramp (steep, left).** Minutes/level ×5 in 40 levels (3.6 → 17.9), then only ×1.8 over Lv.109–200: `XP_STEPS`
   doubles `XP_K` between tiers 19 and 24 and again by 30, while kill XP and kill rate follow smoothly. A designed ramp, not
   a cliff; softening `XP_STEPS` moves every `tierXp` reward too (owner question 6).
3. **Monster damage flat from Lv.117 (flat, left).** `ATK_EASE` cancels the table's atk growth: effective atk 104–108 from
   tier 30 to 50 while hero HP grows 1,241 → 1,789. Probe danger peaks at Lv.49–117 (4.3–4.7× HP/min), drops at the tier-5
   gear step (Lv.149: 2.9×) and stays ~2.1–2.9× to 200. Late threat is HP and ranged kinds only (owner question 3).
4. **No gear step after Lv.149 (flat, left).** 51 levels (≈ 24 h of the 46) on tier-5 base stats; monster HP ×1.6 meanwhile,
   the hero's 48 points add ≈ +12 % HP. Kill rate 15.6 → 11.9/min. Gear between unlocks already trails monster HP
   (weapon atk ×2.3/×1.9/×1.6/×1.5 per step vs mob HP ×2.4/×3/×2.5/×1.6); enhancement, affixes and charms carry the rest.
5. **Ranged kinds halve melee kill rates (matchup, left).** Same tier and XP, single kind, kills/min sword · spear · staff · dagger:
   T28 foxtwine_gold 26 · 21.5 · 28 · 27.5 vs eelknot_frost / brainjelly / owlstare (range 76 volley) 11.5–13.5 · **7.5** · 17 · 21;
   T47 gravrose 8.5 · **2.5** · — · 18.5; T48 crowscare_frost 8.5 · 4 · — · 19.5. The spear is hit hardest: in the
   T42–50 band it kills 5.5–8.5/min vs a 13–21 for the rest (T40 balance band, melee kinds, still ×0.88). Causes T28's dip.
6. **Lv.21–40**: the early-hunt bonus (+60 % to Lv.30, gone at 40) doubles minutes/level Lv.29 → 37 — intended taper.
